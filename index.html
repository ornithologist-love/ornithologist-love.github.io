Recovery & Finally

When a step fails, its dependants are skipped, the Recovery branch runs on that condition, and Finally always runs to release resources.

Inspect a failure → concurrency

Mutex leases & retries

Cross-process mutex rules cap concurrent agent sessions. Transient failures retry with backoff, and the attempt count is shown on the node.

01

Heron · Durable Pregel & local-first automation

A LangGraph-inspired, bulk-synchronous-parallel graph runtime for .NET 8, plus a multi-tenant Go control plane and the Vue 3 console shown above.

  • Durable execution: per-superstep checkpoints, atomic barrier commits and Human-in-the-loop interrupts that resume exactly where they stopped.
  • Coordination: cross-process mutex rules, remote subgraph dispatch with parent-held leases, and recovery and Finally semantics.
  • Operations: Go tenant service for dispatch, schedules and artifacts, with a capability-negotiated Agent protocol.
